Executive Summary
Claimly bridges consumer warranty rights and retail return operations. By deploying Flutter 3.38.9 and Supabase PostgreSQL 17.11 with Deno Edge Functions, Claimly automates receipt encryption, daily expiry surveillance, 0–100 priority triage scoring, 7-day post-expiry grace periods, real-time WebSocket state synchronization, and client-side ISO A4 PDF report generation.

1. Introduction

1.1 Industry Background & Market Dynamics

The global consumer hardware and domestic appliance marketplace exceeds $1.15 Trillion USD annually. Statutory consumer protection statutes and commercial warranties guarantee repair, replacement, or refund remedies for 12 to 60 months. In extended protection plans alone, consumers pay upwards of $85 Billion USD in annual premiums.

1.2 The Consumer Dilemma & Electronic Waste

Despite statutory guarantees, an acute market failure persists: between 40% and 52% of eligible warranty claims are never filed. Consumers absorb out-of-pocket repair costs or needlessly discard functional devices. This dynamic fuels over 53 Million Metric Tons of global electronic waste each year, squandering salvageable hardware due to paper receipt loss and calendar oversights.

1.3 Overview of Claimly

Claimly is an automated warranty and refund claim assistant engineered on Flutter and Supabase. Operating as an autonomous consumer advocate, Claimly transitions warranty management from a fragmented chore into an automated digital workflow:

  • Digital Proof Vault: Encrypted multi-tenant storage for receipts, serial numbers, and invoices.
  • Predictive Expiration Daemons: Daily scheduled cron routines delivering non-intrusive alerts at 30, 7, and 1-day countdown intervals.
  • 5-Step Claim Filing Wizard: Guides consumers through product selection, failure descriptions, evidence uploads, and triage previews in under 120 seconds.
  • Automated Triage Engine: Multi-factor scoring (0–100) detecting safety hazards and enforcing an equitable 7-day post-expiration grace period.
  • Client-Side PDF Compiler: Generates ISO A4 dossiers with SHA-256 QR verification codes directly in client memory in under 400ms.

2. Problem Statement

Traditional warranty workflows suffer from four systemic operational failures:

1. Evidence Degradation & Receipt Loss

Thermal POS paper degrades chemically within 6 to 12 months under exposure to ambient heat, sunlight, and friction. When hardware fails during year two or three of coverage, receipts are completely illegible, resulting in immediate claim rejections.

2. Deadline Lapses ("Expired Yesterday")

Consumers lack automated mechanisms to track purchase horizons. Hardware defects noticed weeks prior to expiration are deferred until after coverage has formally lapsed, permanently forfeiting warranty rights.

3. Submission Friction & Anti-Consumer RMA Portals

Manufacturer portals feature intentional dark patterns: obscure serial code lookups, restrictive upload formats, fragmented brand accounts, and zero visibility into claim progress.

4. Administrative Triage Bottlenecks

Customer support desks receive unstructured claims across disparate channels. Critical safety hazards—such as swelling batteries, sparks, or thermal burns—sit buried beneath routine return inquiries, stalling urgent recalls.

12.2 PostgreSQL Enums, Stored Procedures & Triggers

The schema defines 4 custom enums (user_role, claim_type, claim_status, notification_type), 11 stored procedures, and 14 triggers:

  • handle_protect_user_role: Trigger blocking non-admins from modifying profile role.
  • handle_product_warranty_expiry: Trigger calculating expiration date via interval addition.
  • handle_generate_claim_number: Trigger generating format CLM-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX.
  • handle_claim_status_change: Trigger writing immutable audit rows to claim_timeline.
  • is_admin(check_user_id): SECURITY DEFINER function avoiding recursive RLS loops.

13. Core Logic & Algorithms

13.1 Multi-Factor Priority Triage Scoring (0–100 Scale)

Score = min(100, max(0, Base + ΔUrgency + ΔType + ΔSeverity + ΔCompleteness))

Where:

  • Base: 20 points.
  • ΔUrgency: +30 points if remaining days ≤ 7; +15 points if remaining days ≤ 30.
  • ΔType: +25 points for Refund; +20 points for Replacement; +15 points for Warranty Repair.
  • ΔSeverity (NLP Keywords): +25 points for High-Risk (fire, smoke, shock, burn, hazard, explosion); +15 points for Moderate (broken, failed, dead, cracked, malfunction).
  • ΔCompleteness: +10 points if proof attachments ≥ 1.

13.2 7-Day Post-Expiry Grace Period

Valid ⇔ incident_date ≤ warranty_expiry_date + 7 days

If the incident occurred within 7 days post-expiry, the edge function accepts the claim and sets is_within_grace_period = true, preventing automatic summary rejections.

13.3 Automated Expiry Checker Cron Engine

The cron daemon queries products expiring at T+30, T+7, and T+1 days. Alerts are deduplicated against existing records using a unique composite key:

Deduplication Key = ${user_id}:${product_id}:${milestone}

This guarantees zero duplicate notifications across multiple daily cron invocations.

13.4 Client-Side PDF Compilation with Anti-Fraud QR Hash

The ClaimPdfService generates ISO A4 documents directly in client memory without server round-trips. It embeds a 2D QR Code linking to https://claimly-neon.vercel.app/verify/${claim_number} with a cryptographic verification string:

Hash String = SHA256-${claim.claimNumber}
